# Finger Organ
Play organ chords with your fingers in front of the webcam.

- Live: https://gabiwan24.github.io/airpiano/
- Local: double-click `start.bat`, then click **Kamera & Ton starten**.
- Play hand: 1–5 fingers = I · IV · V · vi · ii of the chosen key. Fist = silence.
- Second hand: 1–5 fingers = 7 · sus4 · sus2 · add9 · major↔minor.
- Hand height = volume (swell). Space = mute / unmute. Without a camera: keys 1–5.
- Tests: `npm test`
